The Ultimate Wedding Budget Checklist: 47 Items You Can't Forget

By Olivia Bennett June 23, 2026 4 min read

The Ultimate Wedding Budget Checklist: 47 Items You Can't Forget

Print this list. Tape it to your fridge. You will forget something. This list will remember.

I have planned 200 weddings. Every single couple forgot at least three items on this list. The marriage license. The cake knife. The tip envelopes. The overnight bag for the hotel. The list goes on.

Venue and rentals (8 items)

Venue rental fee

Ceremony site fee (if different from reception)

Tent rental (backyard weddings)

Tables and chairs

Linens, napkins, table runners

Dance floor

Lighting (string lights, uplighting)

Portable bathrooms (backyard weddings)

Catering and bar (9 items)

Food (per person)

Beverages (alcohol and non)

Service charge (18-22%)

Gratuity (15-20%)

Tax

Cake or dessert

Cake cutting fee

Corkage fee (if bringing your own alcohol)

Late night snack

Photography and video (6 items)

Photographer hourly rate or package

Second shooter

Engagement session

Videographer

Album or prints

Travel fee for photographer

Attire and beauty (5 items)

Wedding dress

Alterations

Suit or tuxedo rental

Hair and makeup (trial included)

Accessories (shoes, jewelry, veil)

Flowers and decor (5 items)

Bridal bouquet

Bridesmaid bouquets

Boutonnieres

Centerpieces

Ceremony decor (arch, aisle petals)

Music and entertainment (4 items)

DJ or band

Ceremony musician

Cocktail hour music

Photo booth

Stationery (3 items)

Save-the-dates

Invitations

Thank-you cards and postage

Gifts and favors (3 items)

Bridal party gifts

Parent gifts

Wedding favors for guests

Logistics (4 items)

Marriage license

Officiant fee

Transportation (shuttle for guests, cars for wedding party)

Day-of coordinator

A "week of" checklist, separate from your budget. Ten days before the wedding, run through this:

Final payments to vendors (cash in labeled envelopes)

Gratuity envelopes (labeled with vendor names)

Marriage license (in your go-bag)

Rings (in someone's pocket, not a drawer)

Vows (printed, in case your phone dies)

Overnight bag for the hotel (change of clothes, toiletries, phone charger)

Snacks for getting ready (you will not eat otherwise)

Comfortable shoes for dancing (your feet will thank me)

Emergency kit (bandaids, safety pins, stain remover, pain reliever, tampons)

Printed timeline for the wedding party (no one checks their email that morning)

Priya forgot her marriage license. She realized the night before. Her county did not issue duplicates on weekends. She had to reschedule the ceremony for Monday. Her out-of-town guests had already flown home. She did a private ceremony later. She still gets sad about it.

Print this checklist. Go through it with your partner. Check off each item. Add the estimated cost. Then open the budget calculator and build your real budget.

Sarah — who forgot the $2,400 — she now sends this checklist to every engaged friend she knows. She says "I do not want anyone else to learn the hard way."
