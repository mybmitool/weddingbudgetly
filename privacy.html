Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

1. No Personal Data Collection

Wedding Budgetly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All budget calculations, savings trackers, and planning tools operate entirely within your web browser. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Local Storage Only

Some tools use your browser's localStorage to save your progress (e.g., wedding savings tracker data). This data is stored locally on your computer and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies to track users. Any cookies set by third-party services (such as advertising networks if enabled) are governed by those services' respective privacy policies.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics that do not collect personally identifiable information. These analytics help us understand which tools are most helpful so we can improve our content.

5. Third-Party Links

Our articles may contain links to external websites (vendor directories, registry services, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@weddingbudgetly.com.
