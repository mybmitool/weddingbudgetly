Frequently Asked Questions

Are Wedding Budgetly tools free to use?

Yes, all our wedding budget calculators, planners, and estimators are completely free to use. No sign-up required.

Is my data saved when I use the calculators?

Some tools like the Wedding Savings Tracker save data locally in your browser using localStorage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. You can clear it anytime through your browser settings.

How accurate are the budget estimates?

Our estimates are based on aggregated industry data from sources like The Knot, Zola, and WeddingWire. They provide a solid starting point, but actual costs vary significantly by location, season, and vendor selection. Always get multiple quotes.

Can I export my budget calculations?

Yes, most tools include an export or print function that lets you save your results as a PDF or share them with your partner, planner, or family.

Do you offer wedding planning services?

No, we do not offer professional wedding planning services or consultations. We provide educational content and free tools to help you plan independently.

How do I reset a calculator?

Every tool page has a "Reset" or "Clear" button that resets all inputs to default values. For the Savings Tracker, you can also clear localStorage in your browser settings.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Absolutely. All tools are fully responsive and work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

What is the average wedding budget in 2026?

Based on our aggregated data, the average wedding cost in 2026 is approximately $36,000. However, this varies dramatically by region, guest count, and style. Our tools help you plan for your specific situation.

Do the tools work for destination weddings?

Yes. The Honeymoon Budget Planner and Wedding Budget Calculator both support destination scenarios. You can input travel costs, accommodation, and currency differences to get accurate estimates.

How do I compare DIY vs professional vendor costs?

Use our DIY Savings Calculator. It compares professional vs DIY costs for flowers, invitations, cake, and decor while factoring in your hourly time value so you see the true cost.

Is there a way to track my wedding savings progress?

Yes, our Wedding Savings Tracker lets you log weekly savings, visualize progress with charts, and see how close you are to your goal. All data is stored locally in your browser.
