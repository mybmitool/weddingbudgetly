How the DIY Savings Calculator Works

This DIY wedding savings calculator answers the question every Pinterest-scrolling bride asks: is DIY actually cheaper? For each project—invitations, flowers, cake, decor, favors, signage—you enter the professional quote, your DIY material cost, and the hours you will spend. The tool computes your real hourly savings rate and gives a verdict: "Worth It!" if you are saving $50+ per hour, "Maybe" for $20–$50, or "Not Worth It" below that. Practical tip: most couples overestimate their DIY savings because they forget to count time, tool rentals, and failed attempts. A $500 professional cake vs. $80 in ingredients sounds like a $420 win, but if it takes you 12 hours and you are not a baker, your hourly rate drops to $35—and that is before stress. Add unlimited projects, compare them all in one bar chart, and export the full analysis so you can decide where to DIY and where to hire a pro.
