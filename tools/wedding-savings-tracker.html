How the Wedding Savings Tracker Works

This wedding savings tracker turns your big-day goal into a weekly chore you can actually stick to. Enter your total wedding budget, what you have already saved, your target wedding date, and a weekly deposit target. The tool calculates exactly how many weeks you have left and what you need to save each week to hit zero remaining. If you are asking how much to save for wedding per week, the answer depends on your timeline: a $30,000 wedding with 50 weeks left means $500 a week, but the tracker adjusts automatically if you miss a week or add a lump sum. Practical tip: set up automatic weekly transfers from your checking to a dedicated savings account so the money disappears before you can spend it. Every deposit is logged in localStorage with a running total, and you can export a PDF-style report to share with your partner or parents.
