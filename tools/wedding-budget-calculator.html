How This Wedding Budget Calculator Works

This free wedding budget calculator helps you figure out exactly how to use your wedding budget calculator to split every dollar across venue, catering, photography, flowers, and more. Enter your total budget and move the sliders to see a real-time wedding budget breakdown by category. The math follows industry averages: roughly 40–50% for venue and catering, 10–15% for photography, and the rest divided by priority. If your allocations go over 100%, the calculator flags it instantly. Practical tip: build in a 10–15% buffer for hidden fees like service charges and overtime—most couples underestimate those. You can also save multiple scenarios to localStorage, compare a Saturday vs. Friday plan, and export your final breakdown as a text report.
