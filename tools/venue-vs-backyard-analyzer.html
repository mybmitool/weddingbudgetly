How the Venue vs. Backyard Analyzer Works

Deciding between a venue vs. backyard wedding is not as simple as "free lawn vs. rental fee." This analyzer pulls in real rental costs—tents, tables, portable bathrooms, generators, lighting, and dance floors—to show you the true all-in price of a DIY backyard setup. On the other side, it loads venue base costs plus hidden fees like coordination charges, cake-cutting fees, and corkage. The result is a side-by-side total that answers the question: is a backyard wedding cheaper? Practical tip: for guest counts under 75, backyards often win by $5,000–$10,000. For 100+, the gap shrinks because rental costs scale fast. Use the comparison button to model luxury venue vs. standard venue vs. backyard in one click, and save your favorite scenario to localStorage.
