How the Wedding Loan Calculator Works

This wedding loan calculator runs the exact amortization math lenders use, showing your monthly payment, total interest, and full payoff timeline. Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and term in months. The tool instantly computes the EMI formula and renders a principal-vs-interest pie chart plus a month-by-month amortization table. If you are wondering how much is a wedding loan really going to cost, the answer is usually 15–30% more than the principal once interest piles up. Practical tip: before you borrow, use the "savings per week" field to see how many weeks of saving would replace the loan entirely. You can also compare 12-, 24-, 36-, 48-, and 60-month terms in one click to find the least painful repayment plan. Export the full schedule to keep your bank account honest.
