How the Honeymoon Budget Planner Works

This honeymoon budget planner by destination builds a complete cost forecast from flights to meals to activities. Choose your destination—Mexico, Hawaii, Europe, Southeast Asia, Japan, or a Caribbean cruise—then set your travel style (budget hostel, moderate hotel, luxury resort, or all-inclusive). The tool applies real per-night cost databases and multiplies by your trip length, number of travelers, and departure-city flight multiplier. If you are wondering how much does a honeymoon cost, the average in 2026 runs $3,000–$8,000 depending on destination and length, with accommodations eating 35–50% of the total. Practical tip: all-inclusive resorts look expensive upfront but often beat à-la-carte pricing if you plan to drink cocktails by the pool. Use the "Compare Destinations" button to see every option side-by-side, save your favorite plan to localStorage, and export a day-by-day budget breakdown to share with your travel agent.
